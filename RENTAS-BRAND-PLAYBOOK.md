# Rentas Brand Concept & Playbook

## Core idea

**Rentas**

**EVERYWHERE IS A ROUTE.**

Rentas is a running space for people who want to start, return to running, or give direction to an existing routine. The brand should feel confident, active and welcoming — never intimidating or overly technical.

## Locked logo direction

- Primary wordmark: **Rentas**
- Capital **R**: Rentas blue
- Lowercase **entas**: deep black or white on dark backgrounds
- Supporting tagline: **EVERYWHERE IS A ROUTE.**
- Font direction: clean geometric sans-serif, bold, upright and easy to read
- Keep the logo simple and scalable
- The tagline is optional for small applications

## Colour system

| Role | Colour | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rentas Blue | \`#2052D9\` | Capital R, buttons, highlights and key brand moments |
| Rentas Ink | \`#151719\` | \`entas\`, headlines, body text and dark backgrounds |
| White | \`#FFFFFF\` | Reverse logo, contrast and clean space |
| Soft Grey | \`#F6F7F9\` | Tool surfaces and supporting backgrounds |
| Warm Yellow | \`#F1D9A7\` | Lock states and small attention markers only |

Use blue with white and ink. Avoid gradients, glow and unnecessary colour effects.

## Typography

The selected direction is **Option 2**:

- Modern geometric sans-serif
- Bold but not overly condensed
- Upright, stable and highly legible
- Slightly customised letterforms where practical, especially the \`R\`, \`a\` or \`s\`
- Use clear spacing so \`Rentas\` reads as one wordmark

The final production logo should be redrawn as vector artwork. The concept image is a direction reference, not the final artwork.

## Do

- Keep the logo clear, flat and easy to recognise
- Use the blue R as the main visual anchor
- Keep generous clear space around the logo
- Use the tagline on posters, campaigns and larger brand moments
- Test the logo on white, black, blue fabric and small social formats
- Keep the wider brand inclusive: **All paces are welcome.**

## Do not

- Do not use a slash, forward slash, arrow or greater-than symbol in the logo
- Do not add a literal road, map pin, shoe or runner icon
- Do not use gradients, glow, bevels, 3D effects or shadows
- Do not make the R much larger than \`entas\`
- Do not force the tagline into every small logo application
- Do not make the brand look like a calculator, CRM dashboard or generic tech product

## Poster and campaign direction

Every Rentas poster or visual concept should start from the same system:

- One strong message or training idea
- \`Rentas\` wordmark with the blue R
- High contrast using blue, ink and white
- Bold headline with simple supporting copy
- Clear hierarchy and generous space
- Confident, direct language
- Route language can appear as layout, movement or story — never as a forced logo symbol

Useful brand messages:

- **EVERYWHERE IS A ROUTE.**
- **ALL PACES ARE WELCOME.**
- **NO PLACE IS SACRED.**
- **Lari ikut kemampuan. Latih dengan tujuan.**

## Training signature marker

The **★** is reserved for Rentas signature training sessions. It is a separate training-system marker and is not part of the primary logo.
